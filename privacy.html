Privacy Policy

Rclone Storage Helper is a personal utility used to facilitate cloud storage access through rclone.

Google Account Access

The application uses Google OAuth authorization to allow rclone to access Google Drive resources according to the permissions granted by the account owner.

Data Usage

Google Drive data is accessed solely for the purpose of enabling cloud storage operations through rclone. The application does not sell or share personal data with third parties for advertising or commercial purposes.

Data Storage

Authentication credentials and tokens are handled by rclone and stored according to the user's local rclone configuration.
